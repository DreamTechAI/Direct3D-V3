Method

Framework.

Direct3D-V3 removes the VAE and performs training and sampling directly in signed distance field (SDF) voxel space, scaling to a resolution of 15363.

Surface Patch Tokenization

Only a small fraction of a high-resolution SDF grid lies near the surface. To exploit this sparsity, we partition the grid into non-overlapping patches and retain those that intersect a coarse mesh. Each retained patch contributes one token containing its raw SDF values. We train the DiT directly on this sparse token sequence.

Dual-granularity DiT

The patch-level DiT combines surface patch tokens with features from the input image. Each patch feature then modulates its voxel tokens at every block of the voxel-level DiT, guiding the prediction of individual SDF values.

Patch-level and Voxel-level DiT

The patch-level DiT provides global semantic structure over sparse surface patches, while the voxel-level DiT restores local SDF variation within each patch. This dual-granularity design avoids applying expensive attention to all voxel tokens directly, yet still preserves the fine geometric detail needed for high-resolution shape generation.
